# Messages

Short, plain, never guilt-tripping. Replace `{braces}`. These are written for
WhatsApp or Discord.

## Outreach (placement or coding group)

> Placements or an internship OA coming up? I'm running a free test for
> 20 people this month.
>
> 30-minute call → I find what you already know, what you can **skip**, and
> your 2 real gaps. Then one 25-minute task a day until you're ready, plus a
> weekly check-in so you can see yourself improve.
>
> No course, no content dump, no AI doing your problems. Just knowing where
> you stand.
>
> Sign up (2 min): {form link}

## Outreach (direct message)

> Hey {name}, saw you're prepping for {placements / the X OA}. I'm testing a
> way to cut prep time by checking what you already know first. Free 30-minute
> call, then a daily task till your test. Want in? {form link}

## Booking confirmation

> Hi {name}, you're booked for {day, time}: {meeting link}.
>
> Have a code editor ready (LeetCode playground works). It's not an exam:
> it's to find what you can skip. Nothing is shared with anyone.

## Position-fix result

Send within 2 hours of the call.

> **{name}, here's where you stand** ({destination}, {date})
>
> ✅ **You can skip:** {topics}. That's about {skip %} of the usual prep list.
>
> 🎯 **Your real gaps:**
> 1. {gap 1}: {one line of evidence, e.g. "stalled choosing a structure on 2 of 2 problems"}
> 2. {gap 2}: {evidence}
>
> 🔍 **Not checked yet:** {unknown core topics}. We'll cover them in your
> weekly check.
>
> 📅 **Rough ready-by:** {date range} at {hours}/week. Your test is {date}.
> {"You're on track." / "That's tight; here's what we'd drop: …"}
>
> This is a rough estimate and it gets sharper each week.
>
> **Tomorrow's task arrives at {time}.**

## Daily "Now"

> **Today · {25} min · {topic}**
> {problem name + link, or a check}
> Why this: {one line, e.g. "it's your top gap and it's in most OAs"}
> Reply ✅ when done, or tell me if you got stuck and where.

Short-on-time variant:

> Only got 10 minutes? Do just {smaller task}.

## Weekly update

> **Week {n} · {name}**
> Moved: {topic}: Gap → Shaky 🎉 ({evidence})
> Still working on: {topic}
> Ready-by now: {range} (was {old range})
> This week's focus: {topic}

## Lapse (after 3 silent days, send once)

> Hey {name}, no pressure. I've rerouted around the missed days. New
> ready-by: {range}. If you've got 15 minutes today: {small task}.

## Ready

> **{name}: you're ready for {destination}.**
> Evidence: {Core topics all Solid}, and you passed the timed mock
> ({score}). Stop adding new topics. Do one light review the day before, then
> go take it. Tell me how it goes. Pass or not, it's the most useful result
> we'll get.

## Pre-pay ask

Send in week 3 to every active student. Ask for real payment, not opinion.

> {name}, this has been a free test, and from next week I'm continuing it as
> a paid season: daily tasks, weekly checks and the ready call, until your
> test on {date}.
>
> It's ₹{price} for the rest of your season. If you want in, pay here:
> {UPI ID / link}. If not, totally fine, and thank you. Either way, please
> tell me your result when you take the test.

Start at **₹499** and note every reaction. If nearly everyone says yes
instantly, the price is too low; if nobody does, ask why before changing it.

## Outcome ask (after their test date)

> Hi {name}, how did {test} go? Even a one-word answer helps: passed /
> didn't / waiting. If you remember, which topics came up?

## Week-3 interview questions (5 minutes, ask everyone, including drop-outs)

1. Was the skip list right? Did it change what you studied?
2. Was there a moment the result felt wrong about you?
3. What would make you stop using this?
4. If this disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
5. Did the checks feel stressful? When?
