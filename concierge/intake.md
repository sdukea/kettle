# Intake form

Build this in Google Forms. Keep it under two minutes.

**Form title:** Pillow: find out what you can skip before your coding interview

**Form description:**
> A free 30-minute check that shows what you already know, what you can skip,
> and your two real gaps before your test. Then one 25-minute task a day until
> you're ready. Run by hand for a small group this month.

## Questions

1. **Name** (short answer, required)
2. **WhatsApp number or Discord username** (short answer, required). How
   we'll send your daily task.
3. **What are you preparing for?** (multiple choice, required)
   - Internship online assessment (OA)
   - Campus placement coding round
   - New-grad interview loop
   - Other (short answer)
4. **Company or companies, if you know** (short answer, optional)
5. **When is the test or interview?** (date, required). If you don't have an
   exact date, pick your best guess.
6. **How many hours a week can you realistically practise?** (multiple choice,
   required): 2–3 · 4–6 · 7–10 · 10+
7. **Which language do you code interviews in?** (multiple choice, required):
   Python · Java · C++ · JavaScript · Other
8. **Roughly how many LeetCode-style problems have you solved?** (multiple
   choice): 0–25 · 25–100 · 100–300 · 300+
9. **What's worrying you most right now?** (paragraph, optional)
10. **Pick a time for your 30-minute call** (Calendly or a list of slots)
11. **Consent** (checkbox, required):
    > I understand my check results are used only to help me prepare and to
    > improve Pillow. They're kept private, never shared with my college or any
    > employer, and I can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

## After the form

- Copy each response into `students` in the private sheet.
- Send the [booking confirmation](messages.md#booking-confirmation) within a
  few hours.
- If someone's test is under 2 weeks away, take them anyway but tell them the
  plan will be a short triage, not a full route.
