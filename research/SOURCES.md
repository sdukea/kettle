# Source Ledger

Every external claim in `research/` cites an ID from this table (e.g. `[S04]`).
All sources were accessed **2026-10-03** unless noted.

**Columns**

- **Type:** `P-research` peer-reviewed or preprint study · `P-data` official
  statistics or survey · `P-voice` a first-person account by a learner,
  engineer or teacher · `P-product` a product's own page or store listing ·
  `S` secondary (journalism, blogs summarizing others) · `V` vendor content
  with a commercial interest in the conclusion.
- **Says:** what the source states, paraphrased. Short quotes are marked.
- **We infer:** our interpretation. This is not what the source says.
- **Conf.:** how much weight the *inference* can carry (High / Med / Low).
